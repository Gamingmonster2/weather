# Earthquakes around Libya: what the numbers mean

Libya is not among the world's most seismically active regions, but earthquakes do occur in and around the country, particularly near the Cyrenaica margin and across the Mediterranean. weather.com.ly lists recent events from the USGS catalog within a wide radius of your selected location.

## How to read an event

- **Magnitude (M)** — a logarithmic measure of energy. Each whole step releases roughly 32 times more energy. Events below M3 are often unfelt; M4+ can be noticeable nearby.
- **Depth** — shallow earthquakes (under ~30 km) tend to be felt more strongly at the surface than deeper ones of the same magnitude.
- **Distance** — how far the epicentre is from the selected city. Local impact depends on both magnitude and distance.

## The feed and its limits

Data comes from the [U.S. Geological Survey (USGS)](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/) FDSN event service. Preliminary solutions can be revised as more stations report, and very small events may appear with a delay or be reclassified.

## Staying informed

- A single moderate quake offshore does not imply a sequence, but it is sensible to stay aware of updates.
- For official guidance and emergency information, follow national and local civil protection authorities.
- Use the map view to understand spatial clustering over the past 30 days rather than focusing on a single reading in isolation.
