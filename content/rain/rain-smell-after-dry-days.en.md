# After a dry spell... why does rain smell?

After a long, dry summer, the first drop falls.

A sweet, earthy smell rises.
Do you know it?
It is the ground taking a breath.

![The ground takes a breath | Raindrops on a window](/assets/articles/glass-rain-city.jpg)

## It has a simple name

Scientists call it **petrichor**.
Do not worry about the word.

It simply means:
the smell of soil after rain.

## Where was that smell?

Dry soil keeps a tiny trace of everything that passed over it.

Sun. Wind. Long days with no rain.

The first drop wakes that trace up.
The air carries it to you.

![The first drop | Wide droplets on glass](/assets/articles/glass-drops-wide.jpg)

## Sometimes you smell it first

The smell can arrive before the clouds reach you.

The wind brings it from farther away.
So you smell the rain before you see it.

The longer the dry spell, the clearer the smell.

## Why does it fade?

After a few hours, it is gone.

The soil is wet now.
The air has carried the trace away.

That is all.

![After the rain | Wet glass up close](/assets/articles/glass-close.jpg)
