# The sea before a storm: read the waves and sky

Before the rain, the sea often changes first.

Do you know the signs?
Three things are enough.

![Clouds over the sea | Storm clouds above the water](/assets/articles/sea-storm-clouds.jpg)

## Sign one: the colour of the sky

Dark clouds come closer.
They gather in one line.
They fill the horizon slowly.

You need no device.
Just look at the horizon.

## Sign two: the waves

The waves grow taller.
They break harder.
They reach farther up the beach.

![Waves under clouds | Big waves under a cloudy sky](/assets/articles/sea-waves-cloudy.jpg)

## Sign three: the wind

The wind suddenly picks up.
It changes direction fast.
It pushes dust or sea spray into your face.

A clear sign:
the weather is changing now.

## What to do

- Stay away from the edge of the waves.
- Do not enter the water when the wind is strong.
- Watch the [Libya rain map](/guides/live-rain-map-libya.html).
- Read the [hourly forecast](/guides/hourly-rain-forecast-libya.html) before you go.

![A Mediterranean sky | Storm clouds over the Mediterranean](/assets/articles/sea-mediterranean-storm.jpg)

## After the storm

The wind calms.
The waves get smaller little by little.
The sky turns blue again.

But the sea can stay rough after the rain.
Wait a while before returning to the shore.
