# Fog and low cloud: why visibility drops

On some mornings, the mountains disappear.

No rain.
The road is wet.
The horizon is white.

![Fog between mountains | Mountains wrapped in fog](/assets/articles/fog-mountains.jpg)

## Fog is a cloud near the ground

It is the same thing.
It just came down to us.

Cool air holds water vapour.
When it cools more, tiny drops appear.
Those drops are fog.

![Cloud on a mountain slope | A hillside surrounded by fog](/assets/articles/fog-slope.jpg)

## Why does it come in the morning?

Night is cold.
The ground loses heat slowly.
The air above it cools too.

Then the first sun warms the air.
The fog lifts a little.
And then it is gone.

## Can radar see it?

Not clearly.

Rain radar does not see fog well.
The drops are far too small.
They do not show as a colour on the map.

So check the [hourly forecast](/guides/hourly-rain-forecast-libya.html).
And look out of the window too.

![A wet street after rain | An empty street between wet buildings](/assets/articles/fog-wet-street.jpg)

## Driving tips

- Drive slowly.
- Use low beam lights, not full beam.
- Leave more space between cars.
- If the fog thickens, stop somewhere safe.

## When does it clear?

Usually one or two hours after sunrise.

In the mountains it may last all day.
Then the view comes back as clear as before.
