# Libya's rain map, made simple

Where is the rain now?
When will it reach you?

Rain.ly answers both.
Look. Then decide.

![It starts with one drop | Raindrops on a window glass](/assets/articles/rain-glass.jpg)

## What you are looking at

Just two layers.

**The base map.** Roads, sea, town names.
This is for finding your way.

**The rain layer.** Coloured patches that move.
This is the part that matters.

Your marker sits on top.
Tap anywhere to move it.

## Colours tell the story

Blue or green? Light rain.
Drive carefully.

Yellow or orange? Moderate rain.
Roads will be wet.

Red or purple? Heavy rain.
Stay out of wadis.

![A storm looks clearer from space | Satellite view of a storm over Earth](/assets/articles/satellite-storm.jpg)

## Watch it move

Radar is not one picture.
It is a short film.

Play the frames.
Ask yourself:

- Where is the band coming from?
- Where is it going?
- Is it growing or fading?

That gives you 30 quiet minutes.
Or 30 minutes to get ready.

## Small mistakes

Do not read roads as weather.
Do not trust a single frame.
Do not expect radar to know everything.
