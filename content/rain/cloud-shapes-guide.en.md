# Cloud shapes: tiny messages in the sky

The sky is talking.
But in a quiet language.

Three shapes are enough to understand it.

![A grey sky | Dense clouds covering the sky](/assets/articles/sky-overcast.jpg)

## Clouds like a blanket

Sometimes clouds cover the whole sky.
They are grey and flat.
They do not move fast.

This is a calm, cloudy day.
It may rain a little.
Or it may stay just cloudy.

## Clouds like cotton

Small white pieces.
They sit in a clear sky.
They move slowly.

They usually mean a lovely day.
But if they grow fast, take care.

![Clouds from above | An aerial view of clouds over Earth](/assets/articles/sky-aerial-clouds.jpg)

## Clouds like a tower

They rise high up.
They look like a mountain of cloud.
Dark base, white top.

This is a heavy rain cloud.
It may bring lightning and thunder.
Find a safe place.

Do not use this guide alone.
Use the [radar](/guides/reading-rain-radar.html) too.

![Clouds at dusk | Pink and blue clouds at sunset](/assets/articles/sky-dusk-clouds.jpg)

## A tiny rule

- Flat and grey → a long cloudy spell.
- Small and white → usually clear weather.
- Tall and rising → watch out for rain.

## Try it today

Look at the sky for one minute.
Pick the closest shape.
Then open the [Libya rain map](/guides/live-rain-map-libya.html) and ask: was the sky honest?
